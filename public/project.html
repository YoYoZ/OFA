How to import in Adobe Premiere Pro

  1. Select your frame rate above and click Download CSV.
  2. Open the Markers panel in Premiere Pro.Window → Markers
  3. Click the panel menu icon (≡) in the top-right corner of the Markers panel.
  4. Choose "Import Markers from CSV…" and select the downloaded file.
  5. Markers appear on your timeline labeled with author name and comment text.
